ABOUT US

Excellence in Dental Care,Built Around You

Dr. Zahers Aesthetic Dental Center is a premium dental clinic in Al Jurf, Ajman, dedicated to providing personalised, high-quality dental care with a strong focus on comfort, precision, and long-term results. Located near Ajman Court, our clinic brings together modern dental technology, experienced clinicians, and a patient-first approach to create a calm and welcoming dental experience.

Our expertise covers a wide range of dental treatments, including dental implants, full mouth rehabilitation, veneers and smile design, root canal treatment, surgical extractions, cleaning, polishing, and whitening. With digital implant planning and carefully planned treatment protocols, we aim to make every procedure as precise, predictable, and comfortable as possible.

At Dr. Zahers Aesthetic Dental Center, we believe that every smile is unique. Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all approach, we take the time to understand each patient's concerns, expectations, and oral health needs before recommending a treatment plan. From your first consultation to your final result, our team focuses on clear communication, transparent treatment planning, and compassionate care.

With more than 12 and 25 years of GCC clinical experience across our dental team, we combine professional expertise with a gentle and unhurried approach. Our dentists provide care in both Arabic and English, ensuring that every patient feels comfortable, informed, and confident throughout their dental journey.

Whether you are looking to restore your oral health, replace missing teeth, improve your smile, or simply maintain healthy teeth and gums, Dr. Zahers Aesthetic Dental Center is committed to helping you achieve a healthier, more confident smile. Book an appointment to begin your journey with us.

Dr. Mohammed Zaher

Dr. Mohammed Zaher

GP Dentist · 12+ Years of GCC Experience

Dr. Mohammed Zaher is a General Practitioner Dentist with more than 12 years of clinical experience in the GCC. His approach combines comprehensive dental care with a strong focus on patient comfort, prevention, and aesthetic results.

His clinical experience includes dental cleaning and polishing, tooth whitening, root canal treatment, surgical extractions, dental veneers, cosmetic procedures, smile design, and smile makeovers.

Dr. Mohammed Zaher believes that effective dental care begins with understanding the patient. By taking the time to explain treatment options and create personalised treatment plans, he strives to make every visit comfortable, transparent, and stress-free.

Dr. Joji Markose

Dr. Joji Markose

Specialist Prosthodontist / PhD Implantologist · 25+ Years of Experience

Dr. Joji Markose is a Specialist Prosthodontist and PhD Implantologist with more than 25 years of clinical experience. His expertise focuses on advanced restorative dentistry, dental implants, complex rehabilitation, and restoring both the function and appearance of the smile.

With extensive experience in implant dentistry and full mouth rehabilitation, Dr. Joji approaches complex cases through careful diagnosis, detailed treatment planning, and modern restorative techniques. His goal is to restore not only a patient's smile, but also their comfort, confidence, and quality of life.
